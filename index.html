Privacy Policy

Solo Traveler — by LimogiAI Solutions
Last updated 31 October 2026

Solo Traveler is a currency converter for travellers. It was built to work without knowing anything about you, and this policy explains exactly what that means.

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your currency list stays on your phone. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not show ads or contain advertising code.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • It does not collect, sell or share your personal information.

What is stored on your phone

So the app opens the way you left it and keeps working without a connection, it saves the following on your phone only:

InformationWhy
Your list of currencies and their orderSo your favourites are there next time
The currency and amount you last enteredSo the converter opens where you left it
The most recently downloaded exchange ratesSo conversion works offline

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Exchange rates

To show exchange rates, the app downloads public rate data, at most a few times a day, from ExchangeRate-API over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The request contains no personal information. As with any internet request, their servers receive standard technical information such as your IP address; ExchangeRate-API's terms cover how they handle it. We do not receive it.

Links to other sites

Tapping "Rates By ExchangeRate-API" in the app opens their website in your browser. This policy does not cover other websites; their own policies apply once you follow a link.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone, so you are always in control: uninstall the app or clear its storage and it is gone. We hold nothing from the app that we could delete for you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the app handles information differently, we will update this policy and the date at the top before that version is released.
