Privacy Policy

Solo Traveler — by LimogiAI Solutions
Last updated 31 October 2026

Solo Traveler is a set of tools for travellers: a currency converter, an EMI and loan calculator, a QR code reader and a place to keep your payment cards. It was built to work without knowing anything about you, and this policy explains exactly what that means.

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything you enter, including saved cards, stays on your phone. Saved cards are encrypted and never backed up, synced or sent anywhere. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not show ads or contain advertising code.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • It does not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • It does not send your card details, loan figures or scanned codes to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your phone

So the app opens the way you left it and keeps working without a connection, it saves the following on your phone only:

InformationWhy
Your list of currencies, their order and the last amount you enteredSo the converter opens where you left it
The most recently downloaded exchange ratesSo conversion works offline
The loan figures you last entered in the EMI calculator and Compare loansSo the calculators open where you left them
Cards you choose to save (see below)So you have your card details to hand while travelling

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Saved cards

If you add a card in My cards, the app saves what you enter: the card number, expiry date and CVV, and optionally the name on the card, a nickname, the bank and notes. This information:

  • is stored encrypted on your phone only. On Android it is encrypted with a key held in the phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). On iPhone it is kept in the iOS Keychain, marked for this device only;
  • is never backed up or synced. The app turns off Android backup (Google cloud backup and transfer to a new phone), and on iPhone the card data is not included in iCloud Keychain and cannot be restored onto another device. If you lose or replace your phone, your saved cards cannot be recovered;
  • is never sent anywhere, to us or to anyone else;
  • is hidden on screen until you choose to show it, and is hidden again when you leave the app. On Android, screenshots and screen recording are blocked while My cards is open. Card numbers and CVVs you copy are cleared from the clipboard after one minute.

Anyone who can use your unlocked phone can open the app and view your saved cards, so keep a screen lock on your phone. You can edit or delete a saved card at any time.

Reading a card with NFC (Android)

On Android phones with NFC, you can fill in a card's number and expiry by holding the card against the phone. The app only reads; it does not make a payment, charge the card or change anything on it. The CVV is not stored on the card's chip, so it is always typed by you. What is read is used only to fill in the form and is saved only if you tap Save.

Camera (QR reader)

The QR reader uses your camera only while it is open, to find QR codes. Camera images are processed on your phone and are never saved, uploaded or shared. The app shows you what a code contains and does nothing with it until you tap an action, such as opening a link, paying with your UPI app or copying text. Those actions open other apps or websites, whose own policies apply.

Exchange rates

To show exchange rates, the app downloads public rate data, at most a few times a day, from ExchangeRate-API over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The request contains no personal information. As with any internet request, their servers receive standard technical information such as your IP address; ExchangeRate-API's terms cover how they handle it. We do not receive it. This is the only network request the app makes on its own.

Backups

On Android, the app is excluded from backups entirely. On iPhone, saved cards are never backed up (see above); other settings, such as your currency list, may be included in your iPhone's own device backup, which Apple encrypts and which only you can restore.

Links to other sites and apps

Tapping "Rates By ExchangeRate-API", opening a link from a QR code, or paying with a UPI app takes you to another website or app. This policy does not cover them; their own policies apply once you leave Solo Traveler.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone, so you are always in control: delete a saved card in My cards, or uninstall the app or clear its storage and everything is gone. We hold nothing from the app that we could delete for you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the app handles information differently, we will update this policy and the date at the top before that version is released.
